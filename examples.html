These are 3 real illustrative outputs from the scanner's new web-search enrichment pass (added 29 July 2026): every company scoring 2/5 or higher on the free Companies House/ORCID/GtR/website-guess signals gets a second, gated pass with Claude's own web_search tool attached, re-checking directors and the company itself against real search results before the brief is finalised. All three below went through that second pass, and nothing here is hand-edited.

VECTIS BIOSCIENCES is the company that originally prompted this feature: the fully automated pass-1 brief scored it 2/5 on Companies House data alone, and it took a human manually checking the company's own website to see the real (fairly sophisticated) product pitch. Correction (30 July 2026): director Alice Condrat does have a real, confirmed ORCID record with a University of Oxford affiliation (orcid.org/0000-0003-4936-4430) -- an earlier version of this page said this couldn't be verified, which was wrong. The root cause: that test never actually ran the pipeline's own ORCID lookup for her at all, and separately, Claude's generic web_search tool -- a different, less precise mechanism than ORCID's own exact-name registry search -- couldn't independently surface her record either. With her real ORCID supplied, pass 1 correctly scores her 4/5 (ORCID confirmations must raise the score, per the scoring rule below); interestingly, adding the web-search pass on top brought it back down to 3/5, since general search still couldn't corroborate anything beyond the ORCID record itself. That's shown below as a real, useful illustration that the two passes don't always agree, not something to paper over.

KYTFOX LIMITED shows the opposite kind of value: search surfaced a genuine, verifiable University of Leeds academic staff page for director Heiko Wurdak (the same page a human reviewer separately found by hand) and an adjacent real project ('Assemblify') the machine had no other way of knowing about.

NON-OXIDE CERAMIC SYSTEMS is the most instructive case: the free signals alone (ORCID-confirmed academic, specialist address) looked promising, but search revealed the two directors are very likely Lucideon's own CEO and Finance Director -- i.e. this reads as an internal corporate vehicle, not an independent startup -- and the score was correctly revised down. In all three cases the system is being appropriately cautious rather than talking things up.
